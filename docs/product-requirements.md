# Product requirements

## Outcome

Deliver a polished independent portfolio demonstration that helps a family understand childcare programs, daily rhythm, illustrative meals and fees, request a fictional tour preview, and find Ottawa's official application process. Give authorized demo viewers a coherent synthetic inquiry-management story.

## Required public experience

Home, Programs, Infant, Toddler, Preschool, Daily Experience, Meals, Fees, About, FAQ with Oasis Guide, Contact, Tours & Registration, Privacy, and Accessibility. English and French navigation and primary content must work at 320, 390, 768, and 1440 pixel widths.

## Tour workflow

Three steps: preliminary guardian contact, non-sensitive childcare interest, then review and separate acknowledgements. The form must not request a child's name, exact birth date, medical/diagnostic record, custody record, identity document, photo, or financial data. Initial mode validates and returns a deterministic reference without persistence or delivery.

## Director workflow

Protected synthetic sign-in, overview, inquiry list/search/filter, inquiry detail, explicit state transitions, assignment, internal notes, immutable-event preview, calendar, bilingual FAQ content, human-reviewed AI proposals, staff, and settings.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

No real operations, real availability, admission prediction, waitlist position, unattended AI actions, remote delivery, child profiles, or affiliation claims. The City of Ottawa registry remains clearly separate.
